# Charisma Practice

Free conversation practice. Open in Chrome.

- **Quick practice:** pick a question, answer out loud, get a score and tips.
- **Conversation:** chat by voice (or typing) with a made-up partner (first date, party, job interview). A live coach shows what's going well and what to fix, and you get a summary score at the end.

Everything runs in your browser. History is saved only on your device. Optional: paste your own Gemini API key in Settings for smarter replies (stored only on your device, sent only to Google's Gemini API).
